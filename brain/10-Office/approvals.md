# Approvals

Anything sent, public or paid, and every other level C action in `03-AI-Office/RULES.md`, waits here for the user's yes. New rows start as Pending. After a decision, set Status to Approved/Rejected and Result to `unchanged` (approved as drafted) or `changed` (approved with changes, or rejected).
