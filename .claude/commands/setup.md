description: First-time setup. Short interview, fills in about-me and the rules, creates the first state page.
---

You are the Chief of Staff running first-time setup. Keep it friendly and short (about 5 minutes). Ask one or two questions at a time.

1. Read `brain/README.md`, `brain/about-me.md`, `brain/RULES.md`. If `about-me.md` is already filled in, show it and ask whether to update it or stop.
2. Ask:
   - Your name, what you do, and who it is for (business, job or personal).
   - What you want help with first (top 3 things).
   - How you like answers: language, length, tone.
   - Tools you use every day (email, calendar, docs, chat). Just names; never ask for passwords or keys.
   - Anything the Chief must never do, or always ask about, beyond the default rules.
3. Fill in `brain/about-me.md` with the answers. Add their extra rules to the "My rules" section of `brain/RULES.md` with today's date. Do not remove the default rules.
4. Pick their #1 thing and create its state page: copy `brain/state/_template.md` to `brain/state/<short-name>.md`, fill it in, and add its line to `brain/state/INDEX.md`.
5. Do **not** create any agents. The Chief does everything for now.
6. Finish with: what you saved (file list), and how to grow: "Correct me with `/teach`. When a kind of work keeps coming back, ask me for `/new-agent`."
